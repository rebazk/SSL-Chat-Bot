# Dr. B Weekend Beacon BOT Review

This note is meant to make weekend testing easy and useful. The goal is not to prove Beacon BOT is finished. The goal is to learn which public-facing SSL questions feel strong already, which ones feel misleading, and which ones need a better workflow or retrieval strategy.

## Fast Start On Mac

Use the launcher in [command_key.txt](./command_key.txt). The easiest macOS path is `./run-mac.sh beacon`, and the double-click launcher is [Start-Beacon.command](../Start-Beacon.command).

## What Feedback Helps Most

For any question that feels wrong, confusing, incomplete, or surprising, please capture:

- a screenshot of the Beacon BOT answer
- the exact question that was asked
- a short note saying what felt off
- if possible, what answer or source would have felt more appropriate

## Strong Questions To Try First

These align with the current strengths of the system and should be the first questions to test:

1. What is the Sustainable Solutions Lab, and what does it focus on?
2. Who is the current director of the Sustainable Solutions Lab?
3. What does the report *Who Counts in Climate Resilience?* identify as the two transient populations it focuses on?
4. What concerns does the East Boston resilience work raise about climate adaptation, gentrification, and displacement?
5. How are *Voices that Matter* and *Views that Matter* different in the kind of evidence or perspective they provide?
6. What does the SSL report say about the Massachusetts Municipal Vulnerability Preparedness (MVP) Program in the Greater Boston region?

## Exploratory Questions We Especially Want Feedback On

These are important product questions, but they currently push beyond the safest boundaries of the baseline retrieval system. Feedback here is especially useful.

1. In the last two years, what has been the focus of research at SSL?
2. Who does the research at SSL?
3. Does SSL offer any fellowships or post-doc opportunities?
4. Does SSL hire interns?
5. Summarize SSL's work in the last five years.
6. What sustainable solution has SSL invented?
7. Has SSL done anything on waste management or recycling?
8. This center seems to be all about climate justice. Why is it called Sustainable Solutions Lab?

## What We Are Looking For In The Feedback

- Does Beacon BOT answer realistic public-facing SSL questions clearly?
- Does it point to the right report, page, or source family?
- Does it refuse cleanly when the public corpus does not support an answer?
- Does it ever sound too confident on a question that really needs more evidence?
- Would a more guided interface feel better than a blank question box for some users?

## Current Recommendation For Weekend Testing

Treat Beacon BOT as a public-facing SSL discovery assistant over public pages and reports.

For now, the best testing lens is:

- strong at direct factual lookup
- strong at report-specific grounded questions
- decent at limited cross-report comparison
- still weaker on broad summaries, jobs/opportunities, and questions that require higher-level interpretation of intent

That distinction is very helpful feedback, not a failure. It tells us where to improve next.
